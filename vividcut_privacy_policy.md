## 1. Overview
At VividCut, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy describes how VividCut handles data when you use our mobile application. VividCut is an artistic typography and graphic design app that lets you create, customize, and export skeleton letter art and cutout designs.

---

## 2. Information Collection and Storage

### Local Storage & Content Creation
* **On-Device Design Storage:** All text cutouts, typography layouts, custom quotes, styles, and draft artwork you create in VividCut are stored **locally on your device** via local app storage. We do not transmit, read, or store your custom creations on external servers.
* **No Account Required:** You do not need to register an account or supply your name, phone number, or physical address to use VividCut.

### Subscriptions and Purchases
* **Google Play In-App Billing:** Purchases (including VividCut Pro monthly and yearly subscriptions) are processed directly by the **Google Play Store**. We do not receive, process, or store your payment card numbers, bank account numbers, or billing addresses. Google provides us solely with anonymized purchase verification tokens to activate Pro features on your device.

### Advertising
* **Ad-Supported Free Tier:** The free version of VividCut may display third-party advertisements served through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). These ad networks may collect anonymized advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) and non-precise diagnostics to deliver non-personalized or personalized ads in accordance with Google Play policy.
* **Ad-Free Option:** Users subscribed to VividCut Pro enjoy a completely ad-free experience.

---

## 3. Permissions
VividCut requests only the minimal permissions required to function:
* `android.permission.INTERNET`: Used solely for Google Play Billing validation and serving advertisements in the free tier.
* `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Used to verify network connectivity before querying Google Play Billing or loading ads.

VividCut does **not** access your contacts, camera, microphone, GPS location, or background sensor data.

## 5. Children's Privacy
VividCut does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The app is rated PEGI 3 / Everyone and contains artistic design tools suitable for general audiences.

---

## 6. Data Deletion & Retention
Because your design data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over it at all times. You can delete any saved cutout design directly from the app interface, or erase all app data by clearing the app cache/data in your Android settings or uninstalling the app.
